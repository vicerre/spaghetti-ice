# Elucidation 075 – Blank Space

Usually, the Storyteller exists in a space outside of that of my characters' home dimensions. In the need that she needs to pull a character in for a chat, however, she is able to manifest a pocket dimension from which she and another character can convene.

This pocket dimension typically exists for no longer than when the Storyteller needs it, and thus, is mostly featureless. The only defining features are her and whoever she brought in, as well as a subtle, constant background of static "snow", much like the fuzzy dark gray people see when they close their eyes. The pocket dimension has no need for any other features, as the Storyteller can conjure anything she needs if needed. Sometimes, there's a set of monitors that watch over the other universes. Sometimes, there's an armchair for her guest.

# Elucidation 076 – The Other Universe

In addition to the universes inhabited by my characters presently, I once entertained a "crossover universe". This universe was much less formal in design, containing characters from a range of early 2000s media and containing nothing resembling an overarching plot. I often resorted at this universe [sic] whenever the storyline of the main universe was on pause.

Characters in this universe included:

- Armin Vicerre (_Spaghetti Ice_)
- Flandre Scarlet (_Touhou Koumakyou ~ the Embodiment of Scarlet Devil_)
- Ryuusei Cartwright (_AdventureQuest_)
- Xerneas (_Pokémon X and Y_)
- Xileen and Talixar (_Kingdom Hearts_ fan characters)

# Elucidation 077 – Vic's Light-Sickle

The cuffs of Vic's jacket are adorned with a set of resonance crystals, a gift and project of sorts from Alis. These crystals act as a substitute for his currently-impaired cryonic abilities, giving Vic the ability to manifest constructs out of hard light.

The form Vic prefers most for these constructs is that of an augmented scythe, or "light-sickle", as he calls it. This construct acts as an extension of the scythe he wielded from when he held cryonic abilities.

Additional details:

- The scythe retains all properties shared by hard light constructs in general:
  - Despite being made of light, this construct has mass.
  - As it is made of light, this construct resists the pull of gravity.
- The scythe has multiple modes of operation:
  - It can be swung like a hammer.
  - It can be fired like a laser gun.
  - It can be mounted as a mode of transportation.
- A scythe is an unwieldy weapon. A scythe as a laser gun is an unwieldier weapon. Yet, the practicality of the light-sickle as a weapon does not matter to Vic, as he chooses the weapon's form for the sake of fun. If Vic truly wanted to win a bout, he could fashion his constructs into much more efficient tools.

# Elucidation 078 – Vic and Alis Relationship

Vic and Alis act as alternate universe counterparts. Due to this status, the two share an unusual relationship with each other.

Roughly speaking, they occupy a space between that of identical twins and identical persons:

- Physiologically speaking, they share enough genetic material to resemble identical twins. Their gene sequences are not 100% identical, however, so they are separate in that regard.
- They share the same mentality, to the extent that they understand each other on a much more fundamental level compared to most other people. Alis knows and manipulates to achieve Vic's desires, and Vic knows which of Alis's buttons to press. The divergence between the two personalities stems from their different formative environments.
