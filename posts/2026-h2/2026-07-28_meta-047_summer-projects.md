# Meta 047 – Summer Projects

Around this time every year, I end up taking on what I informally dub the "summer project". During summer, I find myself in high spirits, so I end up working on an extended project, often lasting around a month.

For reference, the summer projects of prior years were the following:

- [2021] _Spaghetti Ice_ (blog)
- [2022] _Shaved Ice_ (short story)
- [2023] "Breaking Point" Vignettes
- [2024] manga puzzle
- [2025] character settei

This blog has been an exploration, and a very rich one at that. In exploring who these characters are, I uncover who I am, personally. I am at the point in my journey where, as it turns out, the best project I can be working on as of 2026 is... "me".

Given my personal connections to this blog, I've known for a while now that the dissolution of this blog reflects on my need for it as a personal space. I don't think this blog is in danger of dissolving entirely, but I have been finding the need for it less. As it seems, my priorities will shift from character-based projects, at least for the near future.
