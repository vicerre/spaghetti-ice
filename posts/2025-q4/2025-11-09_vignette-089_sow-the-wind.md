# Vignette 089 – Sow the Wind

> It was 8:01 when Alis encountered Armin, hovered over his bed, decked in his nightwear, brandishing a scythe hairs away from Alis's face—again.
>
> "I'm sorry about this," Armin said, "but I need to kill you."
>
> Perhaps unusually enough by now, Alis was set at ease by this statement.

---

> For all of their idiosyncracies, dramatic flourishes such as these always served as a pretense. Armin claimed he needed to confront Alis to protect the flow of the narrative, and while not untrue, Alis deduces the resolution would come about organically enough. Therefore, he sees no reason to decline the invitation.
>
> Before all else, however, Alis needed to gather his bearings for the morning. By his estimation, this would take a minimum of 25 minutes.
>
> Until then, he keeps Armin immobilized.
