# Elucidation 071 – Resonance Crystals

Many of the devices that have recently emerged from Alis's lab come embedded with a distinct visual component—one or more orange, octahedral crystals, referred to as "resonance crystals".

Resonance crystals serve multiple functions in Alis's technology:

- They act as a power source.
- Their resonance properties make them ideal for certain applications.
- They act as a visual icon. Their presence acts as a proxy for Alis's presence.

The use of resonance crystals is inspired by citrine quartz and the use of vibrating quartz in computers to keep track of time.

# Elucidation 072 – Birth Months

Recently, I've given thought to my character's birthdays.

In the past, I've never given too much thought to birthdays. Within the greater context of the universe, birthdays seemed like a trivial bit of data to register. I don't celebrate my characters' birthdays, so I have no significant reason to determine what they should be. If anything, I figured my characters' birthdays would be the date they were conceived, and I no longer have that information.

This has changed slightly, however. My characters are associated with seasons and numbers, and given the interconnected structure of my universe, it stands to reason I can produce meaningful birthdays from these connections. The exact numbers remain unclear, but I can narrow down the range in which my characters' birthdays could be.

| Character | Seasons         | Numbers | Best Birth Months |
| --------- | --------------- | ------- | ----------------- |
| Alis      | winter – spring | 157     | January, February |
| Solana    | summer          | 628     | June              |
| Vic       | autumn – winter | 493     | April, September  |

# Elucidation 073 – Alis and "Yes"

> Fun fact! Alis has a psychological aversion to saying the word "yes". If you ask whether he'd be interested in, say, afternoon tea, he would prefer to respond indirectly, often with a subtle "hmm" sound instead. Go on, check the archives—you'll find this pattern to be true.
>
> You know him by now—he's the person who needed to, at one point, reclaim control over his life. To him, the word "yes" represents a yielding of agency. In other words, he would be allowing someone else to conduct the course of his life.
>
> As of writing, he hasn't felt completely at ease with that possibility, but I'm sure he'll come around to it. There is no possibility that he might snap and nearly cause the complete breakdown of the universe again.

# Elucidation 074 – Ψ-Conductor

Since Alis has accelerated the rate of project development in his lab, he has opted to bring along on his person some of the constructs from those projects.

Among such constructs is a large, Ω-shaped device suspended behind his back, refered to a psionic conductor, or ψ-conductor for short.

The ψ-conductor acts as a catalyst for Alis's already prodigious psionic abilities. Roughly speaking, it allows Alis to extend his abilities over the range of a small city, and with greater speed and potence.

For the most part, Alis doesn't manage this psionic field manually. Instead, he uses a "program" known as Argus for this purpose. Under this role, it acts as an overseer for those under its domain, predicating itself for the next phase of Alis's plan. Originally, this program was known as Oneiros and was responsible for folding people's Headscapes into one another.

Also, as a result of Vic's input, the ψ-conductor makes for a decent chair.

---

In addition to "Argus", I had also considered naming Alis's psionic construct "Keras", after the Greek word for "horn". This would act as a reference to [the gates of horn and ivory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gates_of_horn_and_ivory)) as well as, more cheekily, the [Keras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keras) machine learning library.

Argus pros:

- Better reflects Alis's goals with the construct.
- Personifies the construct, since "Argus" is a name.

Keras pros:

- Parallels Alis better as one associated with ivory and dreams.
- Uses a different first letter from "Alis" and "Armin".
