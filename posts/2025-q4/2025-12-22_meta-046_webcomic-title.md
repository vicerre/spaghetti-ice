# Meta 046 – Hypothetical Webcomic Title

In the hypothetical event I formally construct a webcomic with my characters, I would name the comic _Logical Conclusion_. The term appropriately describes the style of humor that many of my doodles employ, and as a name, it matches the grammatical structure of names such as _Awkward Zombie_ or _Questionable Content_.
